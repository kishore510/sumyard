- Car running costs, monthly penny drift (3d-1, not fixed): yes, the engine rounds each month before summing. `monthly()` (`src/calculators/car-ownership/calc.ts`) takes a twelfth of each year's amount and rounds it to the penny every month, so £400 a year is 48 × £33.33 = £1,599.84 over four years, not £1,600.00 (maintenance £500: £2,000.16). Proposal for the owner: accrue exact twelfths, rounding the running total each month and paying the difference (month m pays round(m × annual ÷ 12) − round((m − 1) × annual ÷ 12) within a year), so each year sums to its annual amount exactly and no month differs from a twelfth by more than a penny. An engine change (F21, F25), so its own session with tests red then green; every car figure would move by pennies. Seen again in 3d-1.1 over a 121-month horizon: insurance at £400 a year shows 121 × £33.33 = £4,032.93, not 121 ÷ 12 × £400 = £4,033.33; maintenance at £500 a year shows 121 × £41.67 = £5,042.07, not £5,041.67 (a twelfth that rounds up drifts up, one that rounds down drifts down, and the drift grows with the months). Proposal unchanged, for the owner to decide: exact twelfths in the engine, the running total rounded once a month as above, so every whole year sums to its annual amount and any part year to its exact share, to the penny.
- **Car flip points, a crossing into a tie (OQ-flip-tie, 3d-1.1, not fixed):** `flipPoints` (`src/calculators/car-ownership/calc.ts`, `search`) returns a real crossing for the savings return, not a clamped search bound, but it stops at the first change of order (F29). The opportunity cost is rounded to the penny, so near a crossing the two totals are exactly level over a narrow band of rates (hundreds of the search's billionth steps), and the order can flicker between level and either option. The first change found is into that band, so the result says `lowerAbove: 'tie'`. In a seeded random search, every one of 169 savings-return crossings in 3,000 cases ended in a tie. The page said "above it, the two are level", which is wrong: past the band one option is lower again (usually the other one). The page now names the option lower below, says the two are level at the threshold, and says the search doesn't tell which is lower above it. Proposal for the owner: have the search carry on past a tie band to the first strict order, and report a crossing only when that order differs from the one below, otherwise no crossing. The same applies to the mileage search. An engine change (F29), so its own session. A skipped test that fails today: `src/calculators/car-decision/results.test.ts`, "OQ-flip-tie".
