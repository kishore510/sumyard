- Car running costs, monthly penny drift (3d-1; **CLOSED in 3e: accepted by the owner as it is, F52**, not changed): yes, the engine rounds each month before summing. `monthly()` (`src/calculators/car-ownership/calc.ts`) takes a twelfth of each year's amount and rounds it to the penny every month, so £400 a year is 48 × £33.33 = £1,599.84 over four years, not £1,600.00 (maintenance £500: £2,000.16). Proposal for the owner: accrue exact twelfths, rounding the running total each month and paying the difference (month m pays round(m × annual ÷ 12) − round((m − 1) × annual ÷ 12) within a year), so each year sums to its annual amount exactly and no month differs from a twelfth by more than a penny. An engine change (F21, F25), so its own session with tests red then green; every car figure would move by pennies. Seen again in 3d-1.1 over a 121-month horizon: insurance at £400 a year shows 121 × £33.33 = £4,032.93, not 121 ÷ 12 × £400 = £4,033.33; maintenance at £500 a year shows 121 × £41.67 = £5,042.07, not £5,041.67 (a twelfth that rounds up drifts up, one that rounds down drifts down, and the drift grows with the months). Proposal unchanged, for the owner to decide: exact twelfths in the engine, the running total rounded once a month as above, so every whole year sums to its annual amount and any part year to its exact share, to the penny.

- **Car flip points, a crossing into a tie (OQ-flip-tie, 3d-1.1; CLOSED in 3d-2, F53):** the search stopped at the first change of order, often into a narrow band where the two penny-rounded totals are level, so it said `lowerAbove: 'tie'`. Per the owner's 3d-2 brief, a search from a strict order now carries on past a tie to the first value where the other option is lower, reports that option above, and the band between as `level: { from, to }`; a touch with the same option lower either side is no crossing. The same applies to the mileage search and the new fuel price search; the horizon value's closed form never had it. The page names the option lower on each side and gives the band in words. The skipped test (`src/calculators/car-decision/results.test.ts`, "OQ-flip-tie") now runs and passes. Near a crossing the order can flicker within pennies, and bisection finds one edge on each side, not necessarily the outermost: each value is said as "about".
