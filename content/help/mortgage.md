The **Mortgage overpayment** page shows what overpaying a mortgage does: the interest saved, the time cut from the term or a lower payment, the balance over time, and, with an early repayment charge, the net saving in pounds and when the interest saved catches up with the charge. It is an illustration of what you enter, not advice. It **uses no tax rules**, so it has no tax year and shows "Uses no tax rules" where other pages show when their tax figures were last verified.

## What you enter

- **Your mortgage**: the **start date** (the day the balance is owed, such as your last statement date; payments fall on that day each month, or a shorter month's last day), the **balance owed**, the **term left** in years and months (your contractual term from your statement or offer, not an estimated end date that already counts your overpayments), and **repayment** or **interest-only**. Dates can be typed as day/month/year or year-month-day.
- **Fixed rate**: switch it off if you are on a variable rate now. On a fixed rate, enter the **rate**, the date it **ends** (payments on or before that date are at the fixed rate), and the **rate after it**. That rate is a guess, assumed to the end of the term.
- **Overpaying**: a **monthly overpayment** from the first payment, and up to ten **lump sums**, each with a date and an amount. A lump sum is paid with the first payment on or after its date, and the page says which. Then choose whether overpaying should **shorten the term** (the payment stays the same) or **lower the payment** (the term stays the same). On an interest-only mortgage neither applies: overpaying lowers what is owed at the end.
- **Early repayment charge**: switch it on if your offer documents show one. Enter the **charge** as a percentage of what you overpay above the **penalty-free limit**, the limit a year (a percentage of the balance, or an amount), and when the charge **ends** (empty: when the fixed rate ends). If your mortgage is already part-way through an allowance year, enter the **allowance used** so far this year and the **payments until it resets**; leave them empty for a fresh year from the first payment.
- **Advanced**: interest worked out **monthly** (a twelfth of the yearly rate on the balance) or **daily** (each day's interest, added on the payment date), with the **day count** for daily interest.

Boxes the results need start empty: the page uses no made-up figures, and lists what is still needed.

## The results

- **Interest saved**, and how long the mortgage takes to clear with overpaying against without ("cleared in 16 years 8 months instead of 19 years 4 months"), is always in view: on a phone in the bar at the top.
- **What overpaying does**: interest saved over the term, how long until the **mortgage is cleared** with overpaying and without, with the time saved and the last payment dates (or, interest-only, what is owed at the end), **total paid** (payments, overpayments and charges) and the **early repayment charge paid**. With a charge, the **net saving** (interest saved less the charge) and the **break-even** date: the first payment from which the interest saved so far stays at least the charge paid so far, or "Not within the term".
- **Payments and balance**: **what you pay each month now** (your payment and your overpayment together), the **contractual payment** worked out from the balance, rate and term left (check it against your statement: if it is far off, the term left may be an estimate that already counts your overpayments), the payment after the fixed rate, the balance when the fixed rate ends and the interest saved while fixed, which does not rest on your guess of the rate after it.
- **Guaranteed return of a pound overpaid**: one return for each rate period, as an effective rate a year, each saying what it is measured against. The one after the fixed rate rests on your guess. There is no blended figure, and no comparison with savings.
- A **chart** of the balance by year without and with overpaying, with the fixed-rate end marked and a table under it, and a **yearly table** below.

## What if

In **What if**, a slider and a box try another monthly overpayment. The results then show it, with your inputs and the what-if side by side. What-ifs are **never saved**: **Reset what-if** drops it, and **Apply to my inputs** puts it into your inputs (with **Undo**).

## Warnings

Warnings are always shown, never hidden: overpaying **over the penalty-free limit**, with the charge for each allowance year (overpayments are never capped at the limit); an overpayment **capped at the balance** (the part not needed is left out); a fixed rate that ends **past the term** or **before the first payment**; and, with daily interest, a schedule whose last payment is far from the regular one: **this schedule drifts under daily interest, so treat the figures as approximate**.

## Not modelled

Product and exit fees, offset, payment holidays and underpayments, more than one product after the fixed rate, trackers, porting, the APRC, overpayments between payment dates, borrowing back, comparisons with savings or ISAs, and tax. Lender terms vary, so check your offer documents; a statement can confirm the figures.
