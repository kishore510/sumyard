## Scenarios

A scenario keeps a calculator’s inputs and tax year under a name you choose, in this browser (a calculator that uses no tax rules, such as compound interest, keeps no tax year). Results are never stored: they are worked out again from the inputs each time, so they always use the calculator as it is now.

On a calculator page, the scenario box at the top shows which scenario is open and whether it has **Unsaved changes**.

- **Save** names and saves the current inputs. Once a scenario is open, **Save changes** updates it and **Save as new** keeps it as it was and saves a copy. **Start new** goes back to the calculator’s starting inputs.
- **Saved scenarios** lists this calculator’s scenarios, with **Load**, **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. Deleting shows a notification with **Undo**.
- **Scenarios** in the menu lists every scenario for every calculator; **Open** takes you to its calculator with it loaded.
- Inputs you haven’t saved last until you close or reload Sumyard.

If a scenario was saved for a tax year this version of Sumyard doesn’t have, it opens with a warning and uses the default tax year instead, until you save it.

## Compare

**Compare** (under Saved scenarios, once you have two) shows two or three scenarios of one calculator side by side: their inputs and results, and for each one after the first, the change from the first. On a phone the table scrolls sideways; the row names stay in view.

## Export and import

- **Export** saves scenarios to a file (`sumyard-scenarios-` and the date): this calculator’s from its page, or all of them from **Scenarios** in the menu. Keep it as a copy, or move it to another device.
- **Import** reads a file like that. Before anything is saved, a preview shows how many scenarios it holds, their names, any that can’t be used and why (for example, a calculator this version doesn’t have), and any with the same name as one you already have. Then choose:
  - **Add**: each is added as a new scenario; exact duplicates of ones you have are skipped.
  - **Replace**: your scenarios are removed and only the file’s are kept. This can’t be undone, so the preview offers an export first.

## Backup and restore

In **Settings**, **Export a backup** saves everything Sumyard keeps in one file: scenarios, settings, theme and whether you have read the “not advice” notice. **Restore from a backup** shows what the backup holds, offers to export your current data first, and replaces your data only when you confirm.

**Clear all data** removes everything Sumyard keeps in this browser. It asks first and offers a backup, because it can’t be undone.

## What is saved in this browser

- your theme choice and your settings;
- your scenarios;
- the last version whose What’s new you opened, for the **New** marker;
- that you have read the “illustrative, not advice” notice.

About Sumyard lists each one and how much space they use. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them, and private or incognito windows usually forget them when closed, so export to keep a copy.
