## What is saved today

Sumyard saves a few small things in this browser, and nowhere else:

- your theme choice;
- your settings (default region, default tax year, and whole pounds or pounds and pence);
- the last version whose What’s new you opened, for the **New** marker;
- that you have read the “illustrative, not advice” notice.

About Sumyard lists each one and how much space they use. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them, and private or incognito windows usually forget them when closed.

## Scenarios (coming)

**Scenarios are not built yet.** The menu shows them as “Coming soon”. When they arrive, a scenario will keep a calculator’s inputs under a name you choose, so you can come back to it or compare it with another. Results will always be worked out again from the inputs rather than stored.

Exporting to a file, to keep a copy or move to another device, is not built yet either; it is coming with scenarios. Until then, what you change is saved in this browser only.
