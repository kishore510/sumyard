Sumyard is a set of personal UK finance calculators in one clean, ad-free place. Its results are illustrative estimates, not financial advice.

## What works today

**Six calculators are here.** [Take-home pay](help:take-home-pay) gives your pay after tax for the year with pension, student loans, benefits and Child Benefit, and a chart of pension contributions ([the optimiser](help:pension-optimiser)). [Compound interest](help:compound-interest) shows how savings could grow with a fee and inflation. [Car decision](help:car-decision) compares keeping a car, buying with cash, a loan, HP or a PCP over the same horizon. [Household budget](help:budget) adds up your income after tax and where it goes. [Mortgage overpayment](help:mortgage) shows what overpaying a mortgage saves, with a fixed rate and any early repayment charge. [Where does the next £X go?](help:next-pound) compares one lump sum into a pension, a mortgage overpayment or an ISA. More calculators arrive in later releases.

- **Calculators**: the home page lists them. Each has its own page and address.
- **Scenarios**: save a calculator’s inputs under a name, load them again, compare two or three, and export or import them. See [Scenarios and saving](help:scenarios-and-saving).
- **Theme**: the button in the top bar cycles through **System**, **Light** and **Dark**. System follows your device.
- **Menu**: the menu button in the top bar opens All calculators (the home page), Settings, Help and what’s new, About Sumyard and Scenarios. The **Sumyard** name at the top also goes to the home page.
- **Settings**: your theme, a default region, a default tax year, how amounts are shown, and backup and restore.
- **What’s new**: the changes in each version. A **New** marker shows until you open it.
- **About**: the version, how much Sumyard stores in this browser, privacy and credits.
- **Tax data**: every tax figure Sumyard will use, with its GOV.UK source and the date it was checked. If a figure a calculator needs isn’t verified, it shows a “not verified” notice instead of results. See [How tax data and “last verified” work](help:tax-data-and-last-verified).

## Getting around

- Help, Settings, About and Scenarios open as a panel over the page: from the bottom on a phone, from the side on a larger screen. Closing the panel takes you back to the page you were on.
- Use the back arrow, or your browser’s Back button, to go back a step. Press `Esc` to step back or close.
- Each page and panel has its own address, so you can bookmark one, for example a calculator or Help.

> Everything stays on this device. Sumyard has no accounts, analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests.
