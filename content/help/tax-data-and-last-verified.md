**Sumyard has no verified tax figures yet.** Every figure is marked **Not yet verified** until it has been checked, and there are no calculators yet.

## Where figures come from

- Each tax year’s rules live in one place in Sumyard, checked against GOV.UK.
- Every figure records the GOV.UK page it came from and the date it was checked.
- A figure that has not been checked stops any calculator that needs it from showing a result, rather than guess.

## The Tax data page

[Tax data](sheet:tax-data) lists each tax year Sumyard has, grouped by topic: income tax, National Insurance, student loans, pensions, Child Benefit and childcare. Each group says how many of its figures are verified. A verified figure shows its value, a link to its GOV.UK source (opens in a new tab) and the date it was checked. You can also open it from About Sumyard.

## The “last verified” label

A label shows a tax year and either **Not yet verified** or **Last verified** with a date. It says Not yet verified while any figure for that year is unchecked; once all are checked, it shows the most recent check. Calculators will show this label next to their results. If a Budget changes the rules, the label tells you whether Sumyard has caught up.

## Your default tax year and region

Settings has a default **Tax year**, labelled “not yet verified” while its figures are unchecked, and a default **Region**. Calculators will start with them, and you will be able to change them in each one. Nothing uses them yet.

> Sumyard’s results are illustrative estimates, not financial advice. Check anything important against an official source.
