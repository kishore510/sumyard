**Every 2026 to 2027 figure Sumyard holds has been checked against GOV.UK**, and the Tax data page shows each one with its source and the date it was checked. There are no real calculators yet; they will use these figures when they arrive.

## Where figures come from

- Each tax year’s rules live in one place in Sumyard, checked against GOV.UK.
- Every figure records the GOV.UK page it came from and the date it was checked.
- Each figure is taken from a GOV.UK page that shows it for that tax year, and checked against a second GOV.UK page where there is one.
- A figure that has not been checked stops any calculator that needs it from showing a result, rather than guess. Instead it shows a “not verified” notice naming the groups of figures it is waiting for, with a link to Tax data. With every 2026 to 2027 figure checked, you would see this only for a tax year added later and not yet checked.

## The Tax data page

[Tax data](sheet:tax-data) lists each tax year Sumyard has, grouped by topic: income tax, National Insurance, student loans, pensions, Child Benefit and childcare. Each group says how many of its figures are verified. A verified figure shows its value, a link to its GOV.UK source (opens in a new tab) and the date it was checked. Income tax bands are shown on income after allowances: each rate applies above the amount shown, up to the next band. You can also open it from About Sumyard.

## The “last verified” label

A label shows a tax year and either **Not yet verified** or **Last verified** with a date. It says Not yet verified while any figure for that year is unchecked; once all are checked, it shows the most recent check. Each calculator shows this label at the top of its page. If a Budget changes the rules, the label tells you whether Sumyard has caught up: a changed figure goes back to not verified until it has been checked again.

## Your default tax year and region

Settings has a default **Tax year**, labelled “not yet verified” while its figures are unchecked, and a default **Region**. Calculators start with the tax year, and you can change it on each one; a scenario keeps the tax year it was saved with. No calculator uses the region yet.

> Sumyard’s results are illustrative estimates, not financial advice. Check anything important against an official source.
