# Optimiser: engine and take-home page

Status: **user-visible since 0.9.0** (slice 1b session 1b-i): the take-home page at `#/c/optimiser` (`definition.ts`, `pageInputs.ts`, `OptimiserPage.tsx`), calling `takeHome`. The engine was built in slice 1a (session F, 0.6.0; G1, 0.7.0; G2, 0.8.0); 1b-i added taxable benefits in kind and post-tax deductions; part 1b-i.1 (0.10.0) added percentage contributions and itemised lists on the page, with no engine change; 0.11.0 added benefits taken from pay (before or after tax), the tax code and PAYE estimate (`paye.ts`) and the Year / Month breakdown (`breakdown.ts`); 0.12.0 redesigned the page's layout with no engine or input change (`sections.ts`, `results.ts`; decisions U1 to U9 in `docs/ui-patterns.md`). Part 1b-ii-a puts `optimise()` on the page (`optimiserInputs.ts`, `chartData.ts`), as a chart below the split bar; 1b-ii-b adds reading the result (`reading.ts`). This file feeds the Help topics (`content/help/take-home-pay.md` and `content/help/pension-optimiser.md`), so it holds no tax figures: they are in `src/config/tax/<year>.ts`, with sources in `docs/verification-log.md`.

## The page (1b-i, 1b-i.1, 0.11.0, 0.12.0, 1b-ii-a)

- **1b-ii-b (reading the result, no engine or stored-shape change):** the page's Year / Month choice drives the chart, its table and the cards below (`perPeriod` in `breakdown.ts`: a month is the annual figure times one over `MONTHS_PER_YEAR`, to the nearest penny; `pageSweepStep(range, 'month')` steps by `DEFAULT_MONTHLY_SWEEP_STEP` a month). `reading.ts`: `currentPoint`, `nearestPoint`, `changeVsNow` (the table's signed "Change vs now" column), `zoomRange` and `pointsIn` (default range from `ZOOM_MARGIN` of the span, at least two steps, below the current contribution to past the furthest threshold point ahead; null for the full range), `costOfMore` (`netCostPerPound` against no contribution, `marginalRate` since the engine's previous point, as pence of a pound), `thresholdWords` (already under, contribute more, a month rounded up, or out of reach with the reason; the full-charge point said plainly), `warningRuns` and `childcareRuns` (every warning of the whole sweep with its runs; eligibility on income only, `CHILDCARE_NOT_CHECKED`), `compareRows` (now against the chosen point). A chosen point (`Contribution to look at`, not stored) is read out above the chart, highlighted, and used by the cost, warnings and compare cards; thresholds are measured from now. The shared `Chart` gained generic `columns` (replacing `rowNotes`), `selectedX` and marker merging (markers closer than a number's width share one number). Compare (`summarise`) shows the version 4 inputs.
- **1b-ii-a (inputs version 4):** `carryForward` (`{ yearsAgo, amount }`, one per year, year order, years 1 to `MAX_CARRY_FORWARD_YEARS`, an input bound), `moneyPurchaseAnnualAllowance` and `checkChildcare`, in the **Pension allowances** section after Pension. The page lists one carry forward box per year of the config's look-back (`carryForwardYears`, through `requireVerified`) plus any stored year beyond it; a year at nothing is dropped. The migration from version 3 adds none of each; take-home, PAYE and the compare summary are identical (compare does not show the new inputs yet). `toOptimiserInputs` is `toTaxInputs` plus the pension always (method, resolved contribution, nothing for none), the savings (resolved employer contribution, carry forward, the flag) and the check; `calculateOptimise` runs `optimise()` at `pageSweepStep(salary + bonus)` (`src/lib/sweep.ts`: the default step, or the smallest multiple of it keeping the sweep within 400 points) and gives a refusal or the input problem, never throws. `optimiserChartData` maps the points to the shared `Chart` in numeric-x mode: spendable or take-home per point, a numbered marker for every point there for a reason other than the sweep (labels joined; with a percentage contribution, its share of pensionable pay), each run of neighbouring cliffs of one kind as one lettered range (the line across it dashed), student loan whole-pound steps as a note, and the marker text as the table's "Marked" column. No label calls a point the best or says which way the curve goes. A refusal takes the chart's place; the assumptions panel adds `optimise()`'s and the chart's not-modelled list.

- **0.12.0 layout (U1 to U9, UI only):** "Your pay" (salary, bonus, where you live, tax year) is always visible; the rest is in `Section`s: Pension, Benefits and deductions, Student loans, Child Benefit, Tax code. `sections.ts` says which stored inputs each holds, whether they differ from the defaults (`sectionsSet`: those open on mount; the page remounts its inputs on a load or a fresh start, so a scenario opens with its own sections open; nothing is stored), which section an invalid field belongs to, and each one-line summary from `resolveInputs` ("Not set" at the defaults; "An input here needs fixing" while one is invalid). `StickyResult` holds the layout: on a phone a sticky bar with take-home for the chosen period, a Year / Month choice (the same choice as the breakdown's) and the badge, collapsing to one line once scrolled past; from `md` two columns, the results sticky. One polite announcement once the inputs settle. Results, top to bottom: warnings, the take-home card (headline for the period; with a supported tax code, "Estimated payslip net pay" from `payslipNet` and "Take-home after the year-end adjustment"), a `SplitBar` of where gross pay goes (`splitSegments`: the breakdown's rows, salary given up and payments after tax together as benefits given up, adding up to gross pay), the breakdown, the PAYE card, "Alongside your pay". Items are compact cards edited in place (Edit, Done), new ones open with the name focused, Remove shows Undo (blocked, with the reason, after a load or when the list is full). `pageResults.snapshot.test.ts` pins the engine outputs, PAYE, the month view and the compare summary for seven inputs from before the redesign.
- **0.11.0 (decisions B10 to B16):** inputs version 3 (the version is the migrations list's length plus one). Benefit items are `{ name, period, value, taken, payment }`; `annualBenefit` multiplies a monthly value and amount taken by `MONTHS_PER_YEAR` and the engine takes them as `TaxInputs.benefits`. `taxCode` is canonical text (`parseTaxCode`) or empty; `calculatePaye` gives `payeEstimate` against the take-home result. The page shows each item's taxable amount and what is taken, the list's totals (taxable, salary given up, paid after tax), a tax code box (an input error for text that is not a code), a PAYE card (the year's estimate, a month, the year's tax, the difference in words, the note on how a difference is settled) or a notice for a code not modelled, a warning when the code's S or C disagrees with where you live, and a Year / Month toggle (Segmented) on the breakdown: the month is `breakdown(annual, 'month')`, rows to the nearest penny, take-home their sum, a penny note when it differs from the year divided evenly, and PAYE for a month alongside (not in the sum). The headline Month figure is the same sum. Migration: version 2 items take their amount as the value with nothing taken; version 1 goes through version 2. Compare adds the salary given up, payments after tax, the code as text, and the PAYE estimate and difference.

- Inputs (`OptimiserPageInputs`, inputs version 2 since 0.10.0): region (default from Settings), salary, bonus (added together as `grossPay`; flexible-benefit cash goes in salary), pension method, the employee and employer contributions (each `{ mode, amount, percentOfPay }`: an amount for the year or a percentage of pensionable pay, both kept so switching loses nothing), pensionable pay (`{ own, amount, includeBonus }`: the salary or the person's own figure, plus the bonus only if included), taxable benefits in kind and post-tax deductions (each a list of `{ name, amount, period }` items, per month or per year), student loan plans, children and Child Benefit claimed or opted out.
- Resolution (`pageInputs.ts`, decisions B6 to B8): a percentage is `percentRatio` of its canonical text times pensionable pay through `mulRound`, to the nearest penny (a modelling decision; GOV.UK states no rule); a monthly item is its amount times `MONTHS_PER_YEAR` (`src/lib/periods.ts`); a list's total is the sum of its items for the year, nothing when empty. `resolveInputs` gives the annual figures; `toTaxInputs` maps them to the engine, which still takes one annual figure each (a pension only above zero); `calculateTakeHome` returns the result, the refusal, or an input problem (a resolved contribution above pay).
- Input warnings (`inputWarnings`, never a cap): a percentage above all of pensionable pay, for either contribution; an item with no amount (a benefit with neither value nor amount taken), by name or place; a tax code for another part of the UK. A negative amount is an input error, as everywhere on the page.
- Migration (B9): version 1's single amounts become amount mode and one yearly item named "Benefits in kind" or "Post-tax deductions" (an empty list for nothing); results are identical. Newer versions are refused. `validateInputs` builds fresh objects, rejects hostile keys anywhere, over-long names, bad periods, modes and percentages, and more than `MAX_LIST_ITEMS` items, and tidies names with `cleanText` (text only).
- The page shows each resolved amount where it is entered: what a percentage comes to, pensionable pay, each item for the year and each list's running total. Items are numbered fieldsets ("Benefit 1", the legend read by screen readers) with a name (`TextInput`), an amount, per month or per year, and Remove; Add is disabled at the limit with the reason.
- Compare (`summarise`): the resolved annual totals, each contribution's mode (and percentage, as a ratio row, when it counts), pensionable pay when a percentage uses it, and each list's item names as text.
- Results: take-home for the year, month and week (the engine's labels: even divisions of the annual figures, so payslips may differ); a breakdown (gross, income tax, NI, student loan, employee pension, HICBC, post-tax deductions, take-home) that adds up; Child Benefit, pension going in, benefits and ANI alongside; engine warnings as notices.
- Assumptions: the engine's list plus the page's own (Child Benefit weeks, pension as contributions only, benefits as entered, the tax code and PAYE estimate, salary sacrifice and after-tax benefits in words, the monthly breakdown, no Class 1A or other employer-side costs in take-home, flex cash as salary, the percentage base and rounding, items added up into one figure, "Monthly amounts are assumed level through the year."; the taper rounding when there are no results).
- Input bounds come from `src/lib/inputLimits.ts` (amounts, children, items per list, name length, the largest percentage); `literals.test.ts` holds `definition.ts` and `pageInputs.ts` to the same no-figures rule as the engine; `OptimiserPage.test.tsx` checks the page, `definition.ts` and `pageInputs.ts` have no pound amounts, percentages or config reads.

## What it does

Take-home pay for one tax year of employment income, with an optional pension contribution and optional Child Benefit, in integer pence (`src/lib/money.ts`):

| Function | In | Out |
|---|---|---|
| `personalAllowance(income, config, policy)` | Adjusted net income | The allowance after the taper, the reduction, the policy used |
| `incomeTax(income, region, config, policy, pension?)` | Gross employment income, `'rUK'` or `'scotland'`, an optional `PensionInput` | Taxable pay, adjusted net income, the band extension, allowance, taxable income, each band's income and tax (with its start after any extension), total |
| `employeeNI(niablePay, config)` | A year's NI-able pay | Main-rate and additional-rate slices, total |
| `studentLoan(plans, pay, config)` | Plans held (`plan1`, `plan2`, `plan4`, `plan5`, `postgraduate`) and pay | Each repayment with its threshold, rate and basis, total |
| `pensionContribution(pension, grossPay, config)` | `{ method, contribution }` and gross pay | What comes out of pay, the provider's relief, what goes in, taxable pay, NI-able pay, the student loan base, the ANI deduction and the band extension |
| `adjustedNetIncome(grossPay, pension, config)` | Gross pay and an optional pension | Net income (step 1), the relief at source deduction (step 3), adjusted net income |
| `hicbc(ani, children, config, policy)` | Adjusted net income and `{ children, payments: 'claimed' \| 'optedOut' }` | Child Benefit due and received, whole steps, percentage charged, the charge, Child Benefit less the charge |
| `takeHome(inputs, config, policy)` | `TaxInputs`: gross pay, region, plans, optional `pension`, optional `childBenefit`, optional `taxableBenefits` and `postTaxDeductions` | Gross, income tax, NI, student loan, pension, HICBC, post-tax deductions, net, Child Benefit and net with it, annual, monthly and weekly, plus the breakdowns, warnings (take-home below nothing) and an assumptions list |
| `annualAllowanceCheck(inputs, savings, config, policy)` | `TaxInputs` and `PensionSavingsInput` (`employerContribution`, `carryForward: { yearsAgo, amount }[]`, `moneyPurchaseAnnualAllowance`) | Threshold income, adjusted income, whether tapered, the reduction and the allowance, carry forward used per year, the MPAA and which limit binds, counted (employee, employer), headroom, excess, the charge, warnings, assumptions |
| `annualAllowanceCharge(excess, reducedNetIncome, region, config, extension?)` | An excess, taxable income after allowances, the region, an optional band extension | Each band's part of the excess and its charge, the total |
| `childcareCheck(grossPay, pension, config)` | Gross pay and an optional pension | Adjusted net income; per scheme the limit, eligibility on income, headroom or amount over; the cliff, warnings, assumptions |
| `thresholdContributions(inputs, config, policy)` | `OptimiserInputs` | Per threshold: the measure, the limit, status (already met, reachable, unreachable and why), the smallest total contribution, the extra over the current one, the measure there |
| `optimise(inputs, config, policy, options?)` | `OptimiserInputs` (`TaxInputs` with a required `pension`, plus `savings` and `checkChildcare`); `{ step, maxPoints }` (default from `src/lib/sweep.ts`) | Candidate points (sweep, current, maximum, thresholds), each with take-home, Child Benefit, the annual allowance charge, spendable, the pension, ANI, threshold income, net cost per pound, marginal rate, the allowance position, childcare, warnings and reasons; the thresholds; the cliffs between points; assumptions |

`ModellingPolicy` holds the choices the rules leave open; `DEFAULT_POLICY` is `{ taperRounding: 'exact', childBenefitWeeks: CHILD_BENEFIT_WEEKS_PER_YEAR }` (`src/lib/periods.ts`). Types are in `types.ts`.

A pension's `contribution` is what comes out of the employee's pay: the salary given up (salary sacrifice), the deduction from gross pay (net pay), or the payment before the provider's relief (relief at source). `grossPay` is always the contractual pay before any sacrifice.

Every tax value is read through `requireVerified`. If any value a function needs is unverified, it returns the typed `Refusal` (the tax year and every unverified path) and no result; the relief-at-source rate is asked for only with relief at source, and the Child Benefit values only with Child Benefit. A negative amount throws `EngineInputError`; an amount that is not a safe whole number of pence throws `MoneyError`; an unknown region, plan, policy, pension method or Child Benefit choice, a child count that is not a whole number, or a gross pension contribution above gross pay throws `EngineInputError`. A config whose bands have no single band at the relief-at-source rate throws an `Error` for relief at source (the band to extend would be a guess).

## Assumptions

Decisions D1 to D7 in `docs/modelling-decisions.md`. In short:

- **Estimates.** Every result is marked as an estimate and carries `assumptions`, plain-language strings for the UI to show.
- **Annual model.** National Insurance and student loans are worked out on the year's pay with the annual thresholds. **Payroll works per pay period**, with weekly or monthly thresholds and rounding in each period, so a monthly payslip can differ from a twelfth of these figures, and a month with a large bonus can differ a lot (more NI at the additional rate that month, a student loan deduction in a month even if the year is under the threshold, and so on). The monthly and weekly views are the annual figures divided evenly, labelled as such, not payslip figures.
- **Marginal bands.** Each band's rate applies to taxable income above the band's start. The personal allowance comes off first.
- **Personal allowance taper.** The published rule, on adjusted net income. Odd amounts: `taperRounding: 'exact'` (default, confirmed by the owner in G1) keeps the exact reduction; `'wholePoundsDown'` rounds it down to a whole pound. They differ by less than a pound of allowance.
- **Adjusted net income** (decision G2). Gross pay, less any salary sacrificed or net pay contribution (neither is taxable pay), less the relief at source contribution grossed up (the guidance's step 3). No Gift Aid or other reliefs. Kept exact, so a gross-up's part of a penny reaches the taper and the HICBC steps unrounded.
- **Pension methods** (decisions G2 and G6, from GOV.UK):
  - *Salary sacrifice:* off taxable pay, NI-able pay (so the student loan base) and ANI; the same amount goes in as an employer contribution. Any extra the employer adds, its NI saving, and the National Minimum Wage floor on a sacrifice are not modelled.
  - *Net pay arrangement:* off taxable pay and ANI; NI-able pay and the student loan base are unchanged. No relief below the Personal Allowance (the low earner's pension payment is not modelled).
  - *Relief at source:* paid from take-home pay; the provider adds relief at the relief-at-source rate (also for a non-taxpayer); taxable and NI-able pay are unchanged; ANI falls by the gross amount; every band above the basic rate band starts later by the gross amount, which gives the higher-rate relief GOV.UK states for a Self Assessment claim (the band's rate less the relief-at-source rate, up to the income taxed at it). The result shows that relief as if claimed in full. The basic rate band is the one band taxed at the relief-at-source rate (PTM044220: the basic rate, or the Scottish basic rate for a Scottish taxpayer); a Scottish starter-rate taxpayer keeps the provider's relief and pays no more.
  - A gross contribution above gross pay is refused, for every method: relief above earnings, and relief at source with no earnings, are not modelled.
- **High Income Child Benefit Charge** (decisions G1 and G4). Child Benefit is the eldest or only child's weekly rate and each other child's, for the whole year, times `policy.childBenefitWeeks`. One percentage point of the Child Benefit received for each whole step of ANI over the threshold (a part step adds nothing), all of it above the full-charge point, rounded to the nearest penny. Opted out: no Child Benefit and no charge. Worked out for this person alone (if a partner has the higher ANI, the partner pays it).
- **Taxable benefits in kind** (decision B1, session 1b-i). The value as entered (the employer's figure, net of anything the employee pays) is added to taxable pay and adjusted net income, so income tax, the taper, the HICBC, childcare and both annual allowance measures see it; not to NI-able pay or the student loan base (Class 1A, never Class 1: NIM13090, CSLM16030). Not cash, so not in gross or take-home. Class 1A is the employer's and not shown. Allowed above gross pay; the pension limit stays gross pay.
- **Post-tax deductions** (B2). Off take-home only, as their own row; no other figure moves. Take-home below nothing is shown with a warning, never capped.
- **Take-home.** Net is gross less income tax, NI, student loan, the pension contribution out of pay, the HICBC and post-tax deductions. Child Benefit received is shown beside it, with net including it. A salary-sacrificed amount counts as out of pay.
- **Rounding.** No intermediate rounding. Each band's tax and each NI rate's contribution to the nearest penny; each student loan repayment rounded down to a whole pound; totals are sums of the rounded parts (`docs/money.md`, "Decided rounding").
- **Student loans.** On NI-able pay, as HMRC's deduction tables say (the same gross pay as for the employer's secondary Class 1 NICs): lowered by salary sacrifice, not by net pay or relief at source (G6). With more than one undergraduate plan, one repayment at the lowest threshold of the plans held; a postgraduate loan is repaid on top; each rounded down on its own.
- **Region.** rUK (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) or Scotland bands. Scottish rates apply to a Scottish taxpayer; the engine takes the region as given.
- **Scope.** Employment income only. The standard Personal Allowance only. National Insurance category A, employee contributions only. One pension contribution, by one method.
- **Child Benefit weeks (G4).** No GOV.UK page prints the count for 2026 to 2027, but HMRC's press release of 31 March 2026 gives yearly amounts equal to 52 weeks of the weekly rates (CB-1), matching `CHILD_BENEFIT_WEEKS_PER_YEAR` from `src/lib/periods.ts`; the policy field allows another count.
- **Annual allowance** (decisions G7 to G11, session G2). Money purchase savings only. Counted: the gross employee contribution (relief at source grossed up, to the nearest penny), a salary sacrifice and other employer contributions. Tapered only if threshold income (gross pay less net pay and gross relief at source; a salary sacrifice added back) **and** adjusted income (gross pay plus employer contributions, whatever the method) are both over their limits; the reduction is rounded down to a pound and the allowance never goes below the minimum. Carry forward: unused amounts are inputs; the current year's allowance is used first, then the earliest year; years beyond the look-back are ignored with a warning; each year's own allowance and membership are not checked. MPAA (flag): applies to the whole year; carry forward is never added to it; the binding limit is the lower of it and the allowance with carry forward. **Never capped:** savings over the limit are reported with the excess, a warning and the charge.
- **The annual allowance charge** (G11). The excess stacked on taxable income after allowances (exact), through the year's bands with any relief at source extension, nothing below the basic rate (the Scottish starter rate does not apply); each band's part to the nearest penny. Reported beside take-home, not taken out of it; `optimise()` subtracts it in "spendable". Who pays (Scheme Pays) is not modelled.
- **Childcare** (G12). This person's exact adjusted net income against each scheme's limit: eligible at the limit, a cliff a penny over (no taper). The partner's income, minimum earnings, the child, immigration status, Universal Credit and vouchers, and England only for 30 hours are not checked.
- **Threshold contributions** (G14). The personal allowance taper start and zero point, the HICBC threshold and full-charge point (with Child Benefit claimed), the childcare limits (if asked) on ANI, and the threshold income limit on threshold income. Closed form in exact pence; relief at source needs the reduction times one less the relief-at-source rate. Salary sacrifice cannot lower threshold income; no employee contribution lowers adjusted income, so it has no threshold point. Under whole HICBC steps, the full-charge point still carries the whole charge; it first falls a penny below.
- **`optimise()`** (G13). Candidate points only: no point is called the best. Spendable = take-home with Child Benefit less the annual allowance charge. Net cost per pound: spendable given up against no contribution, per pound more into the pension. Marginal rate: the share of each extra pound into the pension since the previous point that did not come out of spendable income. Cliffs listed between neighbouring points: HICBC steps, the childcare limit, the taper switching on or off at the threshold income limit, student loan whole pounds (salary sacrifice only). Rounding of each component can also move spendable up by a penny between points a penny apart; that is not listed. Contribution timing within the year is not modelled (annual model).

## Limitations (not modelled)

- Per-pay-period payroll beyond the PAYE estimate's level monthly pay: weekly or monthly NI and loan thresholds, HMRC's own tables and rounding (Tables A to D, not read), week 1 / month 1, bonus months, mid-year starts and leavers, refunds of over-deducted student loan.
- Defined benefit pension savings, the alternative annual allowance (PTM056510 and the MPAA guidance disagree on its figure), first flexible access part-way through the year, Scheme Pays, lump sum death benefits, and carry forward's membership condition and each earlier year's allowance.
- Childcare: the partner's income, minimum earnings and the other conditions; the support's value.
- Employer pension contributions beyond a salary sacrifice, the employer's NI saving, several pensions at once, gross contributions with a claim, Gift Aid, the low earner's pension payment, the relief-at-source no-earnings allowance, the National Minimum Wage floor, the salary sacrifice NICs change from April 2029.
- Child Benefit for part of the year, the partner who pays the charge, the charge through PAYE or Self Assessment.
- Savings, dividends, property, self-employment and other income; benefit exemptions through salary sacrifice, older arrangements' protection, an item that both gives up salary and makes good, a benefit attracting Class 1, benefits not payrolled in the PAYE estimate (it treats every benefit as payrolled, B17), the employer's Class 1A amount; Marriage Allowance, Blind Person's Allowance and other allowances (a code's M or N counts only through its number); K, BR, D, NT, 0T and emergency codes; Welsh rates beyond what the config holds; other NI category letters; employer NI.
- How a student loan repayment is split between plans.
- Any tax year other than the one passed in.
